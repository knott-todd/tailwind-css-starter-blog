## Career goals

My mother has a saying: "Smart for what?" Her point is that keeping your gifts and abilities to yourself is selfish, and you should have a heart of service for your community. That shapes the engineer I want to be: someone who understands the hard problem, then brings other people along to solve it.

Right now, that problem is climate change, because it could literally end the world. After that, I want to work on other UN Sustainable Development Goals, like ending hunger or getting people clean water. The future I want my work to help create is one where our biggest concerns are all first-world problems.

## Goals for my co-op term

These are my SMART goals for my Tesla co-op term, grouped by the skill each one builds.

**Curiosity and imagination**

- Propose one idea every two weeks to improve my team's work life, such as a more efficient SOP or a morale-boosting team activity.
- For every GitHub repository I am assigned, within one week: draw at least one flow diagram of its architecture, ask three questions about why it was designed that way, and suggest one improvement.

**Initiative and entrepreneurship**

- Have a coffee chat with at least one coworker from across the organization every week, to better understand the context of my work.
- By December 13, 2026, write a business case proposal to increase my team's pull request review rate by 10% per month.

**Collaboration and leadership**

- Review at least one of my peers' pull requests every week.
- Plan or attend at least one work social a month with three or more coworkers.
